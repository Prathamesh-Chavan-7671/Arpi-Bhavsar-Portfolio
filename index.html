01

PCG 2026: Peptides & Complex Generics Symposium

28–29 September 2026  |  Aurika, Mumbai

Agenda / programme excerpt
The project

PCG 2026 brought together pharmaceutical industry leaders, regulatory experts, academicians, and technology specialists to examine the evolving landscape of peptides and complex generics. The programme needed to connect emerging science, manufacturing realities, regulatory considerations, and industry priorities in a coherent, industry-focused forum.

Impact and outputs

Helped create a structured forum for cross-sector discussion on the scientific, regulatory, and manufacturing challenges shaping the peptides and complex generics ecosystem. The programme connected multiple stakeholder groups through curated sessions and focused discussions. Post-conference, I am contributing to technical whitepapers that synthesise expert deliberations and translate session insights into longer-form knowledge resources.

My contribution

I played a key role in conceptualising the symposium and translating its scientific and strategic priorities into a structured programme.

  • Scientific programme strategyHelped shape the scientific vision and thematic framework, connecting emerging scientific developments, technological advances, and industry priorities.
  • Agenda and session designLed the development of plenary sessions, technical panels, leadership roundtables, and focused workshops across peptide therapeutics, oligonucleotides, complex generics, bioanalytical characterisation, formulation, drug delivery, and pharmaceutical manufacturing.
  • Speaker and stakeholder engagementManaged identification, outreach, invitations, confirmations, follow-ups, and coordination with industry executives, regulatory representatives, academics, and international experts.
  • Technical content developmentPrepared session briefs, moderator notes, discussion prompts, thematic frameworks, and speaker background documents to clarify session objectives and support substantive discussion.
  • Programme executionAligned speaker requirements, session schedules, stakeholder communications, and logistical needs across teams.
Key outputs

Scientific agenda; session briefs and moderator notes; speaker and stakeholder communications; technical whitepapers in development.

02

Hands-on Training Workshop on Alternatives to Animal Models (3D Cell Culture)

2–5 June 2026  |  ICT Mumbai

Training image / programme excerpt
The project

A four-day hands-on technical training programme designed to provide academic and industry participants with practical exposure to emerging alternatives to animal models, including 3D cell culture, 3D bioprinting, and organ-on-chip technologies.

Impact and outputs

Delivered a structured four-day learning programme that brought together expert instruction, technical demonstrations, and practical training. The workshop supported scientific outreach and capability-building in advanced in-vitro models, helping participants engage with approaches being explored as alternatives to conventional animal-based research.

My contribution

I led the planning, coordination, and execution of the programme, shaping a learning experience that combined scientific context with practical exposure.

  • Programme designDeveloped the four-day structure around key advanced in-vitro technologies and their relevance to New Approach Methodologies (NAMs).
  • Learning flow and contentIntegrated expert-led lectures, technical demonstrations, and practical sessions to connect foundational concepts with hands-on learning.
  • Faculty and technical coordinationCoordinated expert engagement, session schedules, training requirements, and access to relevant technologies, equipment, and technical expertise.
  • Participant coordinationManaged registration-related communications, participant queries, and coordination with academic and industry attendees.
  • On-ground deliveryLed coordination across all four days, overseeing faculty requirements, session transitions, practical activities, and participant engagement.
Key outputs

Four-day training programme; structured lecture and practical-session flow; faculty and participant coordination; hands-on training delivery.
