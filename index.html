From the lab bench to the conference stage

I trained in biotechnology and built monoclonal antibodies against Chikungunya and Dengue using phage display. That taught me how scientists think, and why a good session needs a clear question, the right experts and a sharp brief.

Today I shape the scientific vision of programmes, structure agendas, manage speakers end to end and write the briefs that keep every discussion technically meaningful. I explain peptide therapeutics, regulatory pathways and AI/ML in drug discovery in plain language.
